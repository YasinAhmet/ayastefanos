# Home

The lore vault for **Ayastefanos Utancı**. Everything here comes from the books in `Raw/`: histories, memoirs, diaries and document collections, plus five cleaned-up video transcripts. It follows the [[Rules]].

## Start here
- [[Handbook]]: how to find things, answer a question from the books, and add a new source
- [[Rules]]: how the vault works, including the two protocols, citations and structure
- [[Timeline]]: dated events, each with its source
- [[Soul of the Age]]: what it *felt* like to live in 1873–1919 (built from fiction only)
- [[Sources Ledger]]: every book, its author, its side, when it was written and what it covers
