## Summary

Topkapı Palace stands on the old Byzantine acropolis over the Golden Horn (Mantran). Its Enderun school gave better training than the medreses (Akşin). On 24 November 1922 the new caliph was greeted in its [[Bağdat]] Köşkü (Goloğlu). After 1924 members of the dynasty passed through it on leaving (Bardakçı). Its library holds old proverb collections (Oy).
