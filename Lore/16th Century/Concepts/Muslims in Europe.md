## Summary

Muslims in Europe is the subject of Lucette Valensi's book on Muslims in early modern Europe (French view). She records Maghribi merchants in [[Malta]] and a renegade trader in Tunis in 1622. Mantran notes the Moriscos expelled from Spain, the last wave reaching Algiers and Tunis in 1609. In the 19th century Britain considered what would replace Muslim rule in Europe (Badem). Bardakçı gives the land lost under Abdülhamid: Eastern Rumelia, Cyprus, Egypt and Tunis. In the World War, Austria-Hungary tolerated Muslim prisoners from Russia (Ortaylı).
