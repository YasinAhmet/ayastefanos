# Progressive Republican Party

> **Faction** · Period: [[_Ledger 20th Century (1920-1999)|20th Century (1920-1999)]] · Found in **7** sources on **220** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

The Progressive Republican Party (*Terakkiperver Cumhuriyet Fırkası*) was founded by Rauf, [[Kâzım Karabekir]] and other opponents (Aralov, Russian view; Akşin). Aralov says it used religious slogans and organised revolts, especially in the east. Akşin says it ended with the Sheikh Said revolt, and that with more democracy in 1925 it might have grown. In the 1926 trials some of its leaders, such as [[İzmit]] deputy Şükrü, were accused of involvement in the [[İzmir]] plot ([[Kılıç Ali]]). See [[Sheikh Said rebellion (1925)]].
