# English Friendship Society

> **Faction** · Period: [[_Ledger 20th Century (1920-1999)|20th Century (1920-1999)]] · **Dates:** 1919– · Found in **12** sources on **47** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

The Society of Friends of England (*İngiliz Muhipleri Cemiyeti*) was founded in 1919 under [[Sait Molla]], with the Sultan's support after the [[İzmir]] occupation (Akşin). Atatürk named Vahdettin, Damat Ferit, [[Ali Kemal]], Adil Bey, Mehmet Ali, Sait Molla and the Rev. Frew among its circle in Nutuk (Akşin). Aralov (Russian view) says it was funded by Frew of British intelligence. The video transcripts say Sait Molla watched Kemal Bey's execution from a window. Membership later cost at least one man his pension (Akyıldız, 1930).
