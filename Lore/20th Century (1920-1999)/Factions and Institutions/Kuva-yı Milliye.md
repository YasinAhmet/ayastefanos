# Kuva-yı Milliye

> **Faction** · Period: [[_Ledger 20th Century (1920-1999)|20th Century (1920-1999)]] · **Dates:** 1919–1921 · Found in **18** sources on **506** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

The Kuva-yı Milliye ('National Forces') were the irregular forces of 1919–21. In Western Anatolia they could not stop the Greeks or make serious resistance, while in the south they worked wonders against the French (Akşin). Sinan Paşa's forces retook Karaisalı in March 1920 (Goloğlu). On the Aydın front disorder went on until late 1919, with men like Demirci Mehmet Efe (Goloğlu). Emrence says the Greek defeat by the Kuva-yı Milliye reversed the political process in western Anatolia. Some irregulars, like [[Yahya Kaptan]], did 'many wrongs' in its name (Kansu). They were later merged into the regular army. See [[Çerkez Ethem]], [[Kılıç Ali]], [[Ali Fuat Cebesoy]].
