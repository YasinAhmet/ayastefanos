## Summary

Amasya gave its name to the [[Amasya Circular (1919)]] and to the Amasya talks of October 1919 between the national movement and the Istanbul government (Akşin; Kansu). From Amasya Mustafa Kemal wrote to [[Cafer Tayyar]] on 18 October 1919 (Goloğlu). The American College at Merzifon was in the Amasya district (Ortaylı).
