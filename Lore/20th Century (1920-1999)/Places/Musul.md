# Musul

> **Place** · Period: [[_Ledger 20th Century (1920-1999)|20th Century (1920-1999)]] · Found in **38** sources on **296** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

Musul (Mosul) was a province of northern Iraq. Under Sykes–Picot it fell to France, but Britain later took it (Akşin). After 1918 the 'Mosul question' stayed unsolved (Erdinç), and Rauf listed it among lands occupied after the armistice (Güneş). Allawi (Iraqi view) says [[Faysal]] accepted a precondition as the price for keeping the Mosul province. In 1915 Armenians were deported to the province (Gürün).

   > — [[Irak Kralı I. Faysal (Ali A. Allawi)#p. 611|Allawi, *Irak Kralı I. Faysal*, p. 611]] · *Iraqi source*
