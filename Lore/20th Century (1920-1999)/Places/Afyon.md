## Summary

Afyon (Afyonkarahisar) was the key town of the 1922 [[Great Offensive (1922)]]. A regiment entered Afyon, and Mustafa Kemal announced the victory after two days of fighting (Goloğlu). Its Museum of the War of Independence keeps the memory of 1922 (İnan). The Greeks at Afyon and [[Eskişehir]] had cut the railways (Akşin). Its deputy Ali Çetinkaya was a hard-liner in the Assembly ([[Kılıç Ali]]).
