## Summary

Samsun was the Black Sea port where Mustafa Kemal landed in May 1919 to begin the national movement. In 1919 the British landed troops there (Aralov, Russian view; Kansu). Aralov says the imperialists wanted it as a base against the Soviet Union. Frunze was welcomed there in 1921 (Aralov). Goloğlu records fighting between Çarşamba and Samsun in May 1920. Bardakçı publishes 'Samsun documents'.
