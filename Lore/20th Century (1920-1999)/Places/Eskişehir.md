## Summary

Eskişehir was a railway junction in western Anatolia. In the World War it was one of three economic directorates (Toprak). In 1919–20 the [[Kuva-yı Milliye]] under Fuat Paşa approached it (Kansu), and British-held equipment there was handed to local officials (Akşin). It became a front line in 1921; the Greek presence at [[Afyon]] and Eskişehir cut the railways (Akşin).
