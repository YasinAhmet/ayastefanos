## Summary

Maraş rose against the French occupation in early 1920. A general uprising continued until the French evacuated the city on 11 February 1920 (Akşin). Civilian losses were heavy in [[Antep]], Maraş and [[Urfa]] (video transcripts). In 1895 Armenian rebels of Zeitun cut the telegraph lines to Maraş (Talat's memoir). Güran describes the tribes that summered in the mountains around Maraş.
