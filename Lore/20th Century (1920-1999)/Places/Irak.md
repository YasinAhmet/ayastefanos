## Summary

Irak (Iraq) is treated in the sources as the land of [[Bağdat]], [[Basra]] and [[Musul]]. Allawi (Iraqi view) describes [[Faysal]]'s kingdom under British mandate and the inevitability of long British rule. Religious architecture shaped its art (Mantran). The Mesopotamian front was a relatively quiet defence, though Townshend praised it (Sanders). The Iraqi monarchy ended in the 1958 coup ([[Cemal Paşa]]'s editor; Bardakçı).
