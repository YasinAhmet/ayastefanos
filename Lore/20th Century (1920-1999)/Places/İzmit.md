## Summary

İzmit (Nicomedia) was a port and industrial town near [[İstanbul]]. Its paper factory dates from the 1830s reforms (Ortaylı). In 1915 Armenians of Van were moved to İzmit, and İzmit's Armenians to [[Kütahya]] (Bardakçı). [[Ahmet Anzavur]] had been its sub-governor (Goloğlu). In 1922 Mustafa Kemal met Franklin-Bouillon there. Its deputy Şükrü was a leader in the 1926 plot ([[Kılıç Ali]]).
