# Kütahya

> **Place** · Period: [[_Ledger 20th Century (1920-1999)|20th Century (1920-1999)]] · Found in **33** sources on **188** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

Kütahya was a western Anatolian town. The Egyptian army reached it in the 1830s ([[Cemal Paşa]]; see [[Kavalalı Mehmed Ali Paşa]]). In 1919–20 national cavalry took it and the British withdrew rather than hold the ammunition (Kansu; Akşin). In 1921 [[Çerkez Ethem]] marched towards it against the Western Front (Goloğlu). Güran quotes a document on local notables.
