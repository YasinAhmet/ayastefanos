## Summary

Sivas hosted the [[Sivas Congress (1919)]]. On 2 September 1919 the city celebrated Mustafa Kemal's arrival 'like a unique national feast day' (Goloğlu). [[Kılıç Ali]] says Mustafa Kemal wanted the Sivas movement to be seen as representing the whole nation. Akşin notes that during wartime bread shortages the Sivas mayor, working with flour profiteers, barely escaped lynching. Bardakçı gives Armenian population figures for the province.
