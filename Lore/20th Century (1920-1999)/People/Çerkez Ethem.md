## Summary

Çerkez Ethem led [[Kuva-yı Milliye]] irregulars in the War of Independence (Kansu). He held the Salihli front with forces he armed and fed himself (Goloğlu), fought the Sultan's forces at Geyve and Adapazarı in May 1920 (Akşin), and took Yozgat from the Çapanoğlu rebels (Tonguç). Göztepe even presents his clash with [[Ahmet Anzavur]] as a Circassian tribal rivalry (Akşin). Goloğlu calls him the armed force of the far left, as action was taken against communists.

His forces refused to join the regular army. He showed 'pride and rebellion' (Akşin), resented İsmet, Refet and Fahrettin ([[Kılıç Ali]]), and defied [[Ankara]] on 1 January 1921. Forces sent against him broke his men on 5 January, and he went over to the Greeks, marching on [[Kütahya]] (Goloğlu). A 1935 plot on the Syrian border was believed to be behind him (Akşin).

   > “İnönü de aynı meseleyi doğruladılar: Çerkez Ethem kuvvetlerinin tarihi evreleri bilinir. Bir gün bu kuvvetler Ankara İstasyonu civarında, tam teçhizatlı ve hepsi de atlarına binmiş olarak, toplu bulunuyorlar.”
