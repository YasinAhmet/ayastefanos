## Summary

Ali Şükrü Bey, deputy for [[Trabzon]], was a naval officer and leader of the opposition in the first Assembly. He graduated from the Naval Academy in 1902 (Goloğlu). He wanted a general moral order in the country. With Tunalı Hilmi he resisted in 1920 (Akşin). He argued ministers' posts should be temporary, and led a censure motion against the Interior Minister on 18 May 1922. Argun admired his well-informed criticisms. In March 1923 he disappeared. He had been strangled by men of [[Topal Osman]] Ağa, commander of the Giresun regiment, and Osman was killed in a clash near [[Ankara]] on 5 April 1923 ([[Kılıç Ali]]; Sorguç). Kılıç Ali says that after Sakarya Ali Şükrü had suddenly become friendly with Osman Ağa.
