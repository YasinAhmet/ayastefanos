## Summary

Cafer el-Askeri (1885–1936) was an Iraqi Kurd and German-trained Ottoman officer. He was captured in the 1916 Senussi campaign and then went over to the Arab cause (Murphy). By March 1917 he commanded [[Faysal]]'s regular Northern Army of about 2,000 (later 4,000) men and attacked Ma'an (Murphy; Allawi). He was later military governor of Aleppo, Iraqi Defence Minister at the Cairo Conference, and several times prime minister of Iraq.
