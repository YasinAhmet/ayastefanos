## Summary

Abdülmecid Efendi was the son of [[Abdülaziz]], heir under [[Mehmed VI Vahdettin]], and the last Ottoman caliph. After Vahdettin fled on 17 November 1922, the Grand National Assembly in [[Ankara]] declared that Vahdettin was no longer caliph. On 18 November 1922 it elected Abdülmecid by **148 votes**, 'aged about 45' according to [[İbrahim Sorguç]]'s diary (Goloğlu; Sorguç). He was a caliph without a sultanate ([[Abolition of the Sultanate (1922)]], [[Caliphate]]).

The army marked the event: on 25 November 1922 a parade was held at İnegöl for the oath of allegiance (biat). Murat Bardakçı describes the conditions [[Ankara]] set. He was to publish a message of thanks to the Assembly and openly condemn Vahdettin, and he was addressed only as 'Caliph of the Messenger of the Lord of the Worlds'.

He kept up court style in [[Dolmabahçe Sarayı]]. Nezih Neyzi reproduces an envelope headed in French: 'Palais de Dolma-Bagche, Résidence de Sa Majesté le Calife'. According to François Georgeon, he performed the great Ramazan ceremonies of the old capital for the last time in 1923.

The Caliphate was abolished in March 1924 ([[Abolition of the Caliphate (1924)]], [[Exile of the Ottoman dynasty (1924)]]). [[Kılıç Ali]] notes that when he left, the palace was sealed, with more than 150 people still living in it. In exile he lived at the Palais Carabacel in Nice. According to *Hafız Hakkı Paşa'nın Sarıkamış Günlüğü*, in the 1930s the dynasty split over money claims, and Behiye Sultan joined the faction opposed to him. Sina Akşin mentions reports, which he treats with caution, that [[Mustafa Kemal Atatürk]] had once invited him to [[Ankara]].

   > “Yaşanan bu siyasal altüst oluşların İstanbul'daki Ramazan üzerinde mekanik diyebileceğimiz bir etkisi oldu: Eski Osmanlı başkentinin yüzyıllardır sahne olduğu büyük siyasal ritüeller ortadan kalktı.1 Halife Abdülmecid bu adetleri en son 1923 Ramazan'ında (17 Nisan - 1 6 Mayıs) yerine getirmiştir: Yıldız Sarayı'nda iftar verdi, "huzur derslerine" katıldı, ayın 15'inde mübarek emanetleri ziyaret etti, bayramda da Fatih Camii'nde kıldığı bayram namazından sonra başkentin ileri gelenlerini mutad tebrikler için Dolmabahçe Sarayı'nda kabul etti.”
   > — [[İstanbul'da Ramazan (François Georgeon)#p. 177|Georgeon, *İstanbul'da Ramazan*, p. 177]] · *French source*
5. After his departure in 1924 the palace staff left and all doors were sealed; more than 150 people had lived there.
   > “Abdülmecit Efendi'nin hareketinden sonra, saray erkanı hemen sarayı terk etti ve sarayın bütün kapıları mühürlendi. Sarayda 1 50'den fazla kadın, erkek, bekçi ve haremağası oturuyordu.”
   > — [[Kılıç Ali'nin Anıları (Kılıç Ali)#p. 225|Kılıç Ali, *Kılıç Ali'nin Anıları*, p. 225]] · *Turkish source*
6. In exile in Nice he lived in the Palais Carabacel.
   > “O kararla Nis’i terketmiştim ve bu işlerle uğraşmanın Halife Abdülmecid Efendi’nin Nice’deki sürgün senelerinde yaşadığı Palais Carabacel isimli bina.”
   > — [[Hafız Hakkı Paşa'nın Sarıkamış Günlüğü (Hafız Hakkı Paşa)#p. 137|Hakkı Paşa, *Hafız Hakkı Paşa'nın Sarıkamış Günlüğü*, p. 137]] · *Turkish source*
