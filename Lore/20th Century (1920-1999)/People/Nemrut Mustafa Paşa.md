## Summary

Mustafa Paşa, called 'Nemrut' (Nimrod, i.e. tyrant), was president of the court-martial that tried Unionists in 1919–20, commonly called the 'Nemrut Mustafa Divanı'. Kansu notes he was first a member, then president of it. According to Gürün, Damat Ferit set up the court, and on 10 March 1920 sixty more Unionists were arrested. It sentenced [[Talat Paşa]], [[Cemal Paşa]] and others to death in absentia, and executed several officials. Bardakçı gives two of them: one hanged on 29 July 1920, another on 5 August 1920 ([[Unionist trials (1919-1920)]]).

In the video account he pushed hardest for the execution of the [[Boğazlıyan Kaymakamı Kemal Bey|Boğazlıyan governor]]. He was a member of the Kurdish Teali Society, and was later himself tried by [[Ankara]] as a traitor.

## Interesting details

1. First a member, then president of the court-martial later known as the 'Nemrut Mustafa Divanı'.
   > “Savaştan sonra İttihat ve Terakki üyelerinin yargılandığı Divan-ı Harp Mahkemesi'ne önce üye ardından başkan olarak atandı. Bu mahkeme daha sonra Nemrut Mustafa Divanı adıyla anıldı. Hakkındaki iddialar üzerine görevinden ayrıldı, 1921'de ülkeyi terk etti.”
   > — [[Atatürk'le Beraber (Mazhar Müfit Kansu)#p. 379|Kansu, *Atatürk'le Beraber*, p. 379]] · *Turkish source*
2. Ferit Paşa set up his court; on 10 March sixty more Unionists were arrested.
   > “Ferit Pasha established the court known as Nemrut Mustafa Pasha Martial Court, presided over by Mustafa Pasha, and on 10 March sixty more members of the Union and Progress Party were arrested.”
   > — [[The Armenian File (Kâmuran Gürün)#p. 317|Gürün, *The Armenian File*, p. 317]] · *Turkish source*
3. Talat and Cemal, sentenced to death by his court, fell to Armenian bullets.
   > “Talat Pasha and Jemal Pasha, whom the Nemrut Mustafa Pasha Martial Court had sentenced to death, died by Armenian bullets. Falih Rifki says about Jemal Pasha: ‘Sad Destiny!”
   > — [[The Armenian File (Kâmuran Gürün)#p. 318|Gürün, *The Armenian File*, p. 318]] · *Turkish source*
4. His court's verdict led to an execution in [[İstanbul]] on 29 July 1920.
   > “Nemrud Mustafa Paşa'nın başkanlığındaki divan-1 harbin kararı ile 29 Temmuz 1920'de Istanbul'da idam edildi. Arşivde, Hafız Abdullah Avni Bey'in ailesine verilen mülk ile ilgili belgelere rastlayamadım.”
   > — [[İttihadçı'nın Sandığı (Murat Bardakçı)#p. 30|Bardakçı, *İttihadçı'nın Sandığı*, p. 30]] · *Turkish source*
