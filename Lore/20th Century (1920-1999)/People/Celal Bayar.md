## Summary

Mahmut Celal (Bayar) was born in 1883 in Umurbey, Gemlik, the son of Abdullah Fehmi, a refugee from Bulgaria (Goloğlu). He was the CUP's responsible secretary in [[İzmir]], where he organised the acorn (valonia) producers into a company to resist the big buyers (Toprak). He described the funeral of the executed Kemal Bey, which medical students greeted with wreaths reading 'the great martyr of the Turks' (video transcripts). As a deputy he raised the İzmir occupation in the last Ottoman parliament, but British censorship prevented it from being heard. In [[Ankara]] he was Economy Minister (Goloğlu). Later he was prime minister, co-signed the 'Memorandum of Four' on 7 June 1945 (Akşin), and was president and founder of the Democrat Party (Ortaylı).
