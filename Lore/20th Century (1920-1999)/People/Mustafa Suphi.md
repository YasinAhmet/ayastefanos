## Summary

Mustafa Suphi founded the Turkish Communist Party in Baku in September 1920 (Akşin). Earlier he had been one of the intellectuals exiled by the Unionists ([[Cemal Paşa]]). As a sociologist he argued that past Ottoman rule had stunted both individual and social enterprise (Toprak). On 15 June 1920 he wrote from Baku to Mustafa Kemal (Akşin). In January 1921 he came to Turkey. On the night of 28/29 January 1921 he and his comrades were killed off [[Trabzon]] by Yahya, head of the boatmen, and his men (Akşin; Bardakçı; Goloğlu). Bardakçı quotes the view that the Bolsheviks, having failed to make Anatolia's revolution through him, tried through Enver.
