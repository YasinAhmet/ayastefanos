## Summary

Ahmet Anzavur was a Circassian officer who rose from the ranks, a retired gendarmerie major and former governor of [[İzmit]]. He led the largest royalist revolt against the nationalists in the Marmara region (Akşin, Goloğlu). On 28 October 1919 he telegraphed the Sultan (Goloğlu). He claimed to act in the name of the Şeriat and a 'Fırka-yı Muhammedi' (Akşin), and was backed by the palace, [[Freedom and Accord Party]] and Damat Ferit circles. Finding the field empty, he took Kirmastı, Karacabey and Bandırma, making it his headquarters (April 1920). Akşin calls his revolt a much more violent 'second act'. It was finally cleared from the Marmara basin to Çanakkale (Bardakçı).

According to nationalist accounts (Argun), the British encouraged such risings 'to make Turk kill Turk'. According to the Russian view (Aralov), Britain used here the methods that had failed in Russia.

   > “Nitekim, o sırada Manyas’ta bulunan İzmit eski mutasarrıfı Emekli Jandarma Binbaşısı Ahmet Anzavur Bey, 28 Ekim 1919’da padişaha bir telgraf çekerek, Aydın’ı Yunan işgalinden kurtaracağı vadiyle kurulan Balıkesir Hareket-i Milliye Redd-i İlhak Cemiyeti’nin, son zamanlarda Mustafa Kemal Paşa ile muhabereye girişmesinin, hükümeti devirme arzusunu açıklamasının, valiyi zorla geri çevirmesinin Halifelik ve Sultanlık makamlarına da darbe vurmak isteğinin açık belirtileri olduğunu, bundan çok üzüntü duyan Balıkesir’e bağlı Manyas ve Aşkar bucağı…”
