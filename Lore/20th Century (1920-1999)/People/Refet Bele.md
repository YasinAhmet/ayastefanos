## Summary

Refet (Bele) was a colonel who went to Anatolia with Mustafa Kemal in 1919. In 1917 he commanded three Turkish divisions in an expedition force in Palestine (Sanders, German view). [[Cemal Paşa]] blamed the loss of the Gaza–Beersheba front on Falkenhayn, not Refet. In 1919 he was III Corps commander (Bardakçı). The Istanbul government ordered his arrest with Mustafa Kemal (Kansu). When Mustafa Kemal reached [[Sivas]] he found Refet in [[Ankara]] and brought him to the congress on 7 September (Goloğlu).

He led the force that crushed the Demirci Efe revolt in December 1920 (Akşin). Aralov (Russian view) lists his posts: Defence Minister, corps commander at Dumlupınar, and in the [[Konya]] revolt. After 1922 his troops entered [[İstanbul]] amid celebrations (Bardakçı). In the 1926 [[İzmir]] trials he told [[Cafer Tayyar]] 'let us not hide behind immunity' ([[Kılıç Ali]]). [[Çerkez Ethem]] and his brothers resented him (Kılıç Ali).
