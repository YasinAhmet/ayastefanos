## Summary

Cafer Tayyar (Eğilmez) commanded the I Corps at [[Edirne]] in 1919–20. In 1919 ideas of a neutral government in Thrace up to the Struma were discussed, and Mustafa Kemal wrote to him on Western Thrace from [[Amasya]] on 18 October 1919 (Goloğlu). When the British occupied [[İstanbul]] in March 1920 he refused to delay mobilisation. According to Western sources (Akşin, citing Colonel Rondenay) he declared that the British had ended parliament and called for resistance. He was on the Allied list of commanders to be arrested (Gürün). Later he sat as deputy for Edirne (Kansu). [[Kılıç Ali]] describes his break with Atatürk as 'open and manly'. He was a Progressive Republican, but no link to the 1926 [[İzmir]] plot was found.
