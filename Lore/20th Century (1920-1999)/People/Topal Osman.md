## Summary

Topal ('Lame') Osman Ağa of Giresun was the mayor of Giresun and a militia leader. He had fought as a volunteer with about 100 men on the Caucasus front in the [[First World War]] ([[Kılıç Ali]]). Volunteers under him boarded ship in their national dress with their kemençes (Durgun). In March 1920 the [[Trabzon]] governor went to Giresun on a British torpedo boat to warn him not to touch the Christians (Akşin). Named commander of a militia regiment, he told the Assembly: 'Even if I lose both legs, I will fight on a stretcher until the enemy is thrown into the sea' (Goloğlu). Devoted to [[Mustafa Kemal Atatürk]], he could not stand Ethem's arrogance (Kılıç Ali). The 'Ali Şükrü–Topal Osman affair' of 1923 is noted by Sorguç. (Bouquet's 'Topal Osman Paşa' ancestor of [[Namık Kemal]] is an 18th-century namesake.)

   > “Trabzon Valisi Hamit, Giresun Belediye Reisi Topal Osman Ağa’yı Hıristiyanlara ilişmemesi için uyarmak üzere 18 Mart’ta İngiliz torpidosuyla Giresun’a gitmiş.”
   > — [[Son Meşrutiyet (Sina Akşin)#loc. 514|Akşin, *Son Meşrutiyet*, loc. 514]] · *Turkish source*
4. So devoted to Mustafa Kemal he could not bear Ethem's insolence.
   > “Topal Osman, Mustafa Kemal Paşa'ya o kadar bağlıydı ki, Ethem'in şımarıklıklarına dayanamıyor, her an bir olay çıkarmak istiyordu. Onun Mustafa Kemal'le birlikte gelmek istemeyişini büyük nezaketsizlik ve münasebetsizlik sayıyordu.”

   > — [[Balkan Harbi'nde Ulaştırma (Bülent Durgun)#p. 277|Durgun, *Balkan Harbi'nde Ulaştırma*, p. 277]] · *Turkish source*
