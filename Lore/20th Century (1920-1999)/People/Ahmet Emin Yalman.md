## Summary

Ahmet Emin (Yalman) was a journalist and author of *Turkey in the World War* (1930), one of the vault's sources. He was deported to [[Malta]] with other Turkish journalists (Bardakçı). In 1919 he, like [[Halide Edib]], found an American mandate attractive (Akşin). Nicolle (British view) cites his view that without the mass desertions in the last years of the war, the nation's survival would have been at risk. He supported handing food supply to the army in wartime (Toprak). Later he edited *Vatan* (Bardakçı).
