## Summary

Eleftherios Venizelos came to power in Greece in 1910 and pursued the 'Megali Idea' (Durgun). Before the Balkan Wars, Bourchier used his friendship with Venizelos to build the Balkan alliance (Akşin). In 1914 he talked with Talat about exchanging Greeks of the Aydın coast for Muslims of Macedonia ([[Cemal Paşa]]). Lloyd George admired him (Bardakçı). In 1920 he told Lloyd George's secretary Kerr that Greece could impose the peace on Turkey with British help (Akşin). He lost the November 1920 election, and Constantine came back (Akşin). Gürün quotes the view that he ignored how the occupation of Asia Minor brought new misery to the Armenians. Yalman notes that later he sought to end all disputes with Turkey.
