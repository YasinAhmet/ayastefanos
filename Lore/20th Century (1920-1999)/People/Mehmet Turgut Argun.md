# Mehmet Turgut Argun

> **Person** · Period: [[_Ledger 20th Century (1920-1999)|20th Century (1920-1999)]] · Found in **2** sources on **11** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

Mehmet Turgut Argun was a military veterinarian. His 1921–22 front diary and his 1923 memoir '[[Anadolu]] Hatıra Defteri' form one of the vault's sources, published ninety-one years later with photographs from his album. He was commissioned as a military vet on 28 July 1919. He described himself as a professional and scientist devoted to serving the Turkish nation, and wrote a short life-story in [[İstanbul]] on 7 December 1975. (Mantran's 'Argun' is the 13th-century Ilkhan.)
