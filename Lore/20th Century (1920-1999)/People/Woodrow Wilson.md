# Woodrow Wilson

> **Person** · Period: [[_Ledger 20th Century (1920-1999)|20th Century (1920-1999)]] · Found in **28** sources on **244** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

Woodrow Wilson was US President (elected 1912). His 'Wilson Principles' shaped post-war hopes. Ottoman ministers were asked whether they had backed them before the Bulgarian front collapsed (Erdinç). Nationalists cited them to demand an end to occupations of Turkish areas (Goloğlu). Bardakçı quotes fears that applying them would create Kurdish and Armenian states. He sat with Lloyd George, Clemenceau and Orlando on the Council of Four that gave [[İzmir]] to Greece ([[Kılıç Ali]]; Mantran). Allawi (Iraqi view) says [[Faysal]] was not satisfied after meeting him. [[Cemal Paşa]] sought his help to get food to Syria. Note: several quotes concern other Wilsons, such as the British Colonel Wilson with the Arab army (Murphy).
