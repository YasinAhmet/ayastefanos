## Summary

Mustafa Fevzi (Çakmak) was War Minister in [[İstanbul]] in early 1920 and then Chief of the General Staff in [[Ankara]]. Goloğlu notes that his real name was 'Mustafa Fevzi'. When the British arrested the commander of İstanbul (Ali Sait Paşa) during the occupation, Fevzi Paşa protested to the grand vizier (Akşin). The circulars he had to send telling units to obey British orders were issued, one officer said, 'under the bayonet' (Akşin). According to the Russian view (Aralov), he escaped from İstanbul to Ankara in 1920, telling Aralov that the situation had become unbearable. In Ankara he was War Minister and chairman of the Council of Ministers. On 23 July 1921 he briefed the Assembly on the danger before Sakarya and the revival of the Independence Tribunals ([[Kılıç Ali]]). Mustafa Kemal then made him Chief of the General Staff (Goloğlu). Argun photographs him inspecting artillery in 1922. In 1918 in Palestine, [[Liman von Sanders]] (German view) called him back to Nablus.

Note: other Fevzi Paşas appear: Kaptan-ı Derya Ahmet Fevzi Paşa, who fled to Egypt with the fleet in 1839 (video transcripts), and Ahmet Fevzi Paşa, commander at Balıkesir in 1920 (Kansu).
