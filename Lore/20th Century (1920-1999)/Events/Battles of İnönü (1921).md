# Battles of İnönü (1921)

> **Event** · Period: [[_Ledger 20th Century (1920-1999)|20th Century (1920-1999)]] · **Dates:** 1921 · Found in **11** sources on **33** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

The First Battle of İnönü was fought on 9–10 January 1921 (İnan's chronology). Under [[İsmet İnönü]] it stopped the Greek advance (Kansu). Its success had lasting effects, and İsmet was received in the Assembly (Goloğlu). Proving strength in the west made the Moscow Treaty of 16 March 1921 possible (Akşin). The Greeks were not discouraged and prepared a great offensive (Akşin). The anniversaries of the First and Second İnönü victories were celebrated in the Republic (Neyzi).

2. He won the İnönü battles and stopped the Greek advance.
   > “İnönü savaşlarını kazanarak Yunan Ordusu'nun ilerlemesini durdurdu. Mudanya Ateşkes Antiaşması ve Lozan Konferansı'nda başmurahhas olarak Ankara'yı temsil erti.”
