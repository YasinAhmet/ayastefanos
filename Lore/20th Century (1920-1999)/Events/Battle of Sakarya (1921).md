## Summary

After losing [[Eskişehir]], the Turkish army fell back behind the Sakarya ([[Kılıç Ali]]). The Greek failure at Sakarya was hailed with gratitude (Bardakçı). Officer losses were so heavy that all reserve officers were rushed to the front (Sorguç). Sakarya affected the Unionist exiles abroad, especially Enver's plans (Erdinç). After it a long uncertainty followed while Atatürk prepared the offensive (Akşin). Argun compares the Greeks' later 'tortoise march' to their Sakarya defeat.
