# Opening of the Grand National Assembly (1920)

> **Event** · Period: [[_Ledger 20th Century (1920-1999)|20th Century (1920-1999)]] · **Dates:** 23 April 1920 · Found in **32** sources on **722** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

The Grand National Assembly opened in [[Ankara]] on 23 April 1920 and began governing the country (Argun). See [[Hamdullah Suphi]] for how deputies from the dissolved Istanbul parliament joined it. Fevzi Paşa told it that the Istanbul government had been driven out 'with the bayonet' (Akşin). It sent envoys abroad, such as to [[Berlin]] in 1921 (Kemal). On 18 November 1922 it discussed Vahdettin's flight (Sorguç). Pierre Loti thanked it for thinking of him (Goloğlu). Aralov (Russian view) sent it congratulations on 14 September 1922.

   > “Nitekim Fevzi Paşa TBMM’de, “...    > “22 Kasıqı 1922, Çarşamba Büyük Millet Meclisimizin 18.11.1922 tarihli toplantısında Vahdettin Efendi'nin İngilizlere iltica ederek firar eylediği mevzu bahis olarak hal edilmiş ve yerine Abdülaziz'in mahdumu 45 yaşlarında Abdülmecit Efendi Hazretleri, Büyük Millet Meclisi'nce halife olarak seçilmiş.”
