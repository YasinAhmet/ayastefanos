# Malta exiles (1919-1921)

> **Event** · Period: [[_Ledger 20th Century (1920-1999)|20th Century (1920-1999)]] · **Dates:** 1919–1921 · Found in **30** sources on **252** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

The British deported Ottoman politicians and officers to [[Malta]]. After the occupation of [[İstanbul]] on 16 March 1920, many were arrested for Malta by 10 a.m. (Akşin). A list was sent, except twelve to be held at Mudros (Gürün). [[Ali İhsan Paşa]] and fifteen others escaped (Goloğlu), and [[Kara Kemal]] escaped with nineteen others (Erdinç). [[Ankara]] asked that the exiles be tried in Turkish courts (Kansu). Some later became deputies, such as the [[Kars]] deputy and Free Party founder Akşin mentions. On 20 November 1922 Sultan Vahdettin himself landed on Malta (Bardakçı).
