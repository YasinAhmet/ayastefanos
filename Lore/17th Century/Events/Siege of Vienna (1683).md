## Summary

In 1683 Grand Vizier [[Merzifonlu Kara Mustafa Paşa]] besieged Vienna and was heavily defeated (Tonguç). Trotsky's chronology dates the start of Ottoman decline to this failure. Ortaylı argues the defeat should not be overrated in Turkish history, and that the popular slogan 'Turks before Vienna' refers mostly to the first siege (1529). The shock of 1683 and the losses of Karlowitz shaped later policy (Bardakçı). Ortaylı notes that in 1683 William Penn proposed a European parliament. Kula shows how the siege fed German 'national' literature.
