## Summary

Viyana (Vienna) was the Habsburg capital and a symbol of Ottoman expansion; the slogan of 'Turks at the gates of Vienna' refers mainly to the first siege (Ortaylı). See [[Siege of Vienna (1683)]]. The first Greek newspaper appeared there in 1793 (Ortaylı). Hafız Hakkı served there until 1910 (Hakkı Paşa). In the Crimean War, Russia's envoy Meyendorff failed to win Austria (Badem). In the World War, Ottoman paper money was backed by gold deposited in [[Berlin]] and Vienna (Toprak).
