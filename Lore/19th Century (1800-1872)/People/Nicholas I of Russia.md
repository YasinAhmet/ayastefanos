## Summary

Nicholas I was Tsar of Russia before and during the [[Crimean War (1853-1856)]]. On 9 January 1853 he told the British ambassador that 'we all want the sick man to live' (video transcripts). Gürün says Britain rejected his 1853 proposal to divide the empire (see [[The Sick Man of Europe]]). Yalman notes [[Stratford Canning]] had spoken of decline two years before him. He believed only monarchs ruling 'by the grace of God' deserved to be called brothers (Badem). In August 1849 he demanded the return of Polish refugees, and the Porte ignored him (Ortaylı). He had set up the Erivan province, which the video transcripts call the seed of Armenia. He died on 2 March 1855 and Alexander II sought peace (Gürün; Hobart-Hampden).

## Interesting details

1. 9 Jan 1853: 'We all want the sick man to live…'
   > “9 Ocak 1853'te İngiliz elçisine şöyle der Çar Nikolay: "Hasta adamın yaşamasını hepimiz istiyoruz. Onun ölmemesini ben de sizin kadar isterim, buna inanmanızı rica ediyorum.”
   > — [[Mahşerin İki Gemisi - Part I (Video transcript)#loc. 15|Video, *Mahşerin İki Gemisi - Part I*, loc. 15]] · *Turkish source*
2. Britain did not accept his 1853 proposal to the British ambassador.
   > “It is for this reason that Britain did not accept the suggestion made by Nicholas I, Tsar of Russia, to the British Ambassador in 1853: ‘Well, we have here a sick man, a very sick man; it would be, I tell you frankly, a great misfortune if, one of these days, we were to lose him, especially before the necessary arrangements had been made.’!9 Britain was on the side of the Ottoman Empire during the Crimean War.”
   > — [[The Armenian File (Kâmuran Gürün)#p. 108|Gürün, *The Armenian File*, p. 108]] · *Turkish source*
3. He thought only monarchs 'by the grace of God' deserved to be called brothers.
   > “Çar Nikolay sadece kendisi gibi "Tanrı'nın inayetiyle" iktidara gelmiş olan hükümdarların kardeş olarak hitap edilmeye layık olduklarını düşünüyordu, halbuki Fransa imparatoru sadece halkı tarafından seçilmişti.56 III.”
   > — [[Kırım Savaşı ve Osmanlılar (Candan Badem)#p. 80|Badem, *Kırım Savaşı ve Osmanlılar*, p. 80]] · *Turkish source*
4. August 1849: his demand for the Polish refugees was ignored by the Porte.
   > “Daha Ağustos 1849’da Çar Nikola’nın Sultan Abdülmecid’e yazdiği ve Polonyalıların iadesini talep eden mektup, Babıali tarafından kaale alınmadı”
   > — [[Osmanlı'da Değişim ve Anayasal Rejim Sorunu (İlber Ortaylı)#p. 240|Ortaylı, *Osmanlı'da Değişim ve Anayasal Rejim Sorunu*, p. 240]] · *Turkish source*
5. He died on 2 March 1855; Alexander II wanted to end the war.
   > “Alexander, who ascended the throne after Nicholas I died on 2 March 1855, wanted to put an end to the war, especially after Sebastopol fell on 9 September.”
   > — [[The Armenian File (Kâmuran Gürün)#p. 90|Gürün, *The Armenian File*, p. 90]] · *Turkish source*
