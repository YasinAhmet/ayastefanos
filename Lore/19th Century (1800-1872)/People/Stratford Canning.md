# Stratford Canning

> **Person** · Period: [[_Ledger 19th Century (1800-1872)|19th Century (1800-1872)]] · Found in **9** sources on **140** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

Stratford Canning (Lord Stratford de Redcliffe) was British ambassador in [[İstanbul]]. Mantran calls him one of those who best knew the '[[Eastern Question]]'. From 1842 he spoke of the empire's decline (Yalman). He spoke so harshly to Ottoman statesmen that some Britons said 'the Sultan means Lord Stratford' (video transcripts). He restored [[Mustafa Reşid Paşa]] to office. With his backing the Sultan rejected Menshikov's demands, and Menshikov left İstanbul noisily on 21 May 1853 (video transcripts). Badem notes Slade's reports to him on the navy.

Note: George Canning, British Foreign Secretary and Prime Minister, was a different man. He sympathised with the Greek revolution, which made Metternich call him 'mad' (Ortaylı; Gürün).

   > — [[Mahşerin İki Gemisi - Part I (Video transcript)#loc. 15|Video, *Mahşerin İki Gemisi - Part I*, loc. 15]] · *Turkish source*
2. With his support the Sultan rejected Russia; Menshikov left on 21 May 1853.
   > “İngiliz Büyükelçisi Stratford Canning'in desteğiyle Sultan Rus taleplerini reddetti. Menşikov 21 Mayıs 1853'te büyük bir gürültü kopararak İstanbul'dan ayrıldı; ardından Rusya Osmanlı ile diplomatik ilişkilerini kesti.”
   > — [[Mahşerin İki Gemisi - Part I (Video transcript)#loc. 15|Video, *Mahşerin İki Gemisi - Part I*, loc. 15]] · *Turkish source*
3. French view: Sir Stratford, one of the best experts on the [[Eastern Question]].
   > “9 Mayıs'ta, İstanbul'daki İngiliz elçisi, "Şark Meselesi"ni en iyi bilenlerden biri olan Sir Stratford of Redcliffe, sultanı ziyaretle, Britanya donanmasının olası bir katılımını vaat eder ona.”
   > — [[Osmanlı İmparatorluğu Tarihi (Robert Mantran)#p. 625|Mantran, *Osmanlı İmparatorluğu Tarihi*, p. 625]] · *French source*
4. Metternich called (George) Canning 'mad' for his Greek policy.
   > “Canning, Yunan İhtilâli'ne sempati duyan bir politika izlemeye başlayınca, muhafazakâr politikanın şampiyonu Metternich, Canning'i, "deli, kaçık" diye nitelemiştir.”
   > — [[Osmanlı'da Milletler ve Diplomasi (İlber Ortaylı)#loc. 165|Ortaylı, *Osmanlı'da Milletler ve Diplomasi*, loc. 165]] · *Turkish source*
