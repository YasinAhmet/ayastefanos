# Ömer Paşa

> **Person** · Period: [[_Ledger 19th Century (1800-1872)|19th Century (1800-1872)]] · Found in **19** sources on **121** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

Müşir Ömer (Lütfi) Paşa was commander on the Danube in the [[Crimean War (1853-1856)]] and later in the 1862 Montenegrin war (Bouquet). His ultimatum to Gorchakov in October 1853 began the war (Badem). He was later sent to the Caucasus, where Oliphant said he had good reason to know the local people helped the enemy (Badem). He was governor in Bosnia in 1860–61 (Mantran). Kula quotes Victor Hugo's poem 'Lazzara' on 'What would Ömer Paşa give?'. Note: other Ömer Paşas appear, such as Vahdettin's aide who went into exile with him in 1922 (Goloğlu) and Dr. Besim Ömer Paşa (Neyzi).
