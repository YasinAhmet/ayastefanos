# Sources Ledger

A list of every source in `Raw/`. Books are sorted by the period they cover.

- **Side**: the source's point of view, set by the author's country. Ottoman-era authors count as Turkish. For edited diaries, memoirs and letter collections, the side is the person who wrote the original, not the editor.
- **Written**: when the work was first written or published in its original language. The edition in the vault is often a much later Turkish translation or reprint.
- **Covers**: the years the book is about.
- `~` means approximate.
- **Files**: the format of the copy kept in the vault. Duplicates were moved out (see [[#Duplicates]]).

Where things are:
- `Raw/NormalTrust/`: all 69 books, one file each.
- `Raw/MostTrusted/`: 5 cleaned-up video transcripts (listed at the end). The raw auto-generated subtitles they were made from are in `Raw/MostTrusted/_Subtitles/`.
- `Raw/_Duplicates/`: extra copies of books, kept for reference.
- `Raw/Doubtful/`: empty.
- `Converted/`: every book as Markdown, with a `p. N` (or `loc. N`) heading per page, so notes can link straight to a page. Books with chapters have `## Chapter` headings and `### p. N` pages.
- `Raw/Converted (original)/`: the converted texts as they were before the 2026-09-24 cleanup (`.txt`).
- `Raw/OCR corrections/`: one list per book of every word the OCR corrector changed, plus `_page quality.tsv` (pages that are still hard to read).
- `Raw/_Snapshots/`: a zip of the whole vault taken before the cleanup.
- `Attachments/Images/`: illustrations from Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons (⚠ not from vault sources), credited in [[Image credits]].
- `Sources/`: one note per book (`Source - <Title>`), listing every vault note that quotes or mentions it.

- **#40 Childs**: treated as American, but not confirmed from the file.
- **#24 Ryan**: an Australian surgeon who served *with* the Ottoman army. The side is Australian, but his view is sympathetic to the Ottomans.
- **#41 Hochwächter, #45 Liman von Sanders**: German officers who served in the Ottoman army. Their view is that of an ally, not a neutral observer.
- **#65 Fidan**: a Turkish author, but the book is mostly translated Chinese travel accounts of Turkey.

## Video transcripts (`Raw/MostTrusted/`)

These are YouTube videos in Turkish. Each file was rebuilt from the auto-generated subtitles: punctuated, split into paragraphs and sections, and with misheard names corrected where they could be identified with confidence. Anything that couldn't be recovered is marked `[anlaşılmıyor]` (unclear), and uncertain readings are marked `[?]`. Every file starts with a header that lists its topic, period and editing notes. The claims in the videos are the speakers' own and were not fact-checked.
