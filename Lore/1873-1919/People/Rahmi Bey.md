# Rahmi Bey

> **Person** · Period: [[_Ledger 1873-1919|1873-1919]] · Found in **16** sources on **133** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]


## Summary

Rahmi (Arslan) was a member of the CUP Central Committee and governor of [[İzmir]] in the war. Yalman says he felt independent enough to all but ignore the war. When an epidemic threatened, he gave a German doctor wide powers despite Turkish doctors' objections (Sanders, German view). In 1918 he offered himself as a channel for peace feelers through İzmir (Bardakçı). A French-citizen merchant family of İzmir figures in claims about his contacts. He was arrested and deported to [[Malta]] (Talat's memoirs). Talat says men like Rahmi had been against the German alliance. [[Kılıç Ali]] says [[Çerkez Ethem]] kidnapped Rahmi's young son for ransom during the armistice. He was later tried in 1926 (Erdinç).
