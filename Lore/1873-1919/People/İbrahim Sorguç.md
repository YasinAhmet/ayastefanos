# İbrahim Sorguç

> **Person** · Period: [[_Ledger 1873-1919|1873-1919]] · **Dates:** d. 26 Jul 1974 · Found in **1** sources on **31** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

İbrahim Ethem Sorguç was an officer whose War of Independence diaries and [[First World War]] memoirs, written 30 years later, make up one of the vault's sources. His son Erdoğan Sorguç edited them. He fought on the Palestine front and then in Anatolia. His belongings from his 'war chest', including a Japanese bandage packet found on the field after the Commander-in-Chief's battle, are shown in the Harbiye Military Museum. He received the Independence Medal in 1931, wore uniform a third time in the Second World War, and died in [[Ankara]] on 26 July 1974 aged 77. He stayed loyal to Mustafa Kemal all his life and kept far from politics.
