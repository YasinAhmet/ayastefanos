## Summary

Hüseyin Nazım Paşa was War Minister and acting commander-in-chief in the [[Balkan Wars (1912-1913)]]. Akşin says he bore main responsibility for the defeat, while political responsibility lay with [[Gazi Ahmed Muhtar Paşa]] and [[Kamil Paşa]], who kept him there. On 1 November 1912 he wanted a political settlement and doubted the Çatalca line. Earlier letters to the palace show him calling himself the Sultan's 'faithful slave and devoted servant' (Akşin). He was shot during the [[Raid on the Sublime Porte (1913)]]. Grosser-Rilke says he had been forced out by [[Enver Paşa]] and 'shot'. A witness in Bardakçı insists nobody meant to kill anyone that day. [[Cemal Paşa]], made commandant of [[İstanbul]], organised a grand funeral for him the next day.

Note: other Nazım Paşas appear: a governor of Aydın in 1914 (Toprak), Mustafa Nazım Paşa who presided over the 1919 deportation trials (video transcripts), and others in 1919 (Kansu).
