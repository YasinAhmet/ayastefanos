## Summary

Sir Mark Sykes was the British negotiator of the [[Sykes-Picot Agreement (1916)]] with François Georges-Picot of France (Murphy; Mantran). The talks of May 1916 were later extended to Russia (Mantran). The agreement divided the Arab lands into British and French zones; it was published by the Soviet government after the October Revolution ([[Cemal Paşa]]). Akşin notes that Mosul fell to France under it but was later taken by Britain. In 1915 he discussed Prince Sabahaddin's plan for a coup and peace (Akşin). He met [[Faysal]] at Wejh on 2 May 1917 (Allawi).
