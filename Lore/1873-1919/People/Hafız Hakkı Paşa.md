## Summary

Hafız (İsmail) Hakkı Paşa was a staff officer and one of the planners of the [[Sarıkamış Operation (1914-1915)]]. Bardakçı says he was born in [[Manastır]] in 1879, the son of a cavalry lieutenant, and was in Vienna until December 1910. In 1910 he married a granddaughter of [[Murad V]]. In 1914 he and Halil were sent to German headquarters to argue for six more months of Ottoman neutrality ([[Cemal Paşa]]). His diary, published in *Hakkı Paşa*, records how the war began on 29 October 1914.

From Hasankale on 22 January 1915 he wrote to [[Enver Paşa]] that if they entered Russian land they could raise the Muslims there. His own words show him as one of the architects of [[Sarıkamış]] and so one of those responsible for the rout. [[Liman von Sanders]] (German view) called him one of the most distinguished officers of the Turkish General Staff, and he took over the shattered Ottoman Third Army from Enver. He quarrelled with the Baghdad governor Süleyman Nazif (Jan–Feb 1915). He died of typhus on 12 February 1915 (Sanders; Tonguç).
