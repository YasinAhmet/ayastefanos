# Mehmet Şevki Yazman

> **Person** · Period: [[_Ledger 1873-1919|1873-1919]] · **Dates:** 1896– · Found in **4** sources on **14** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

Mehmet Şevki Yazman was born in 1896 in Germili village, Elazığ. He studied at Erzincan military school and in [[İstanbul]], and served on the [[Galician Front (1916-1917)]]. His memoir *Mehmetçik Avrupa'da* was first printed in 1928. The editor says it shows that war was not monotonous: soldiers lived courage, fear, joy, worry, fun and even love in those hard days. He was elected Democrat Party deputy for Elazığ in 1950 and served two terms, including as deputy speaker.
