# Charles S. Ryan

> **Person** · Period: [[_Ledger 1873-1919|1873-1919]] · Found in **8** sources on **63** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

Dr. Charles Ryan was an Australian surgeon who served with [[Gazi Osman Paşa]]'s army at Plevne in 1877. He was the only Briton in that army and was nicknamed 'Plevna Ryan' (Hobart-Hampden). His memoir *Plevne'de Bir Avustralyalı* (1897) is a source in this vault (Australian view). He reached [[İstanbul]] in mid-1876, deeply impressed by the 'legendary eastern capital'. He later became Sir Charles in Melbourne.

Note: many quotes about 'Ryan' concern Sir Andrew Ryan, chief dragoman of the British Embassy in 1919–22. Andrew Ryan dealt with Vahdettin and Damat Ferit, saw Vahdettin with High Commissioner Rumbold on 6 April 1922, and wrote *The Last of the Dragomans* (Akşin; Bardakçı). Gürün notes his known hatred of the Turks.
