## Summary

Yakup Cemil was one of the Unionist volunteer gunmen. He fought against the Italians in Libya ([[Italo-Turkish War (1911-1912)]]), and during the [[Raid on the Sublime Porte (1913)]] he shot the war minister [[Nazım Paşa]]. According to Akşin, Nazım cried 'you deceived me!' as he was shot, and the rumour was that the CUP had won his support by promising him the grand vizierate. Childs (American view) corrects the details of the scene.

In 1916 he was accused of plotting against the government and was executed. Grand Vizier Said Halim told the parliamentary inquiry he was 'a madman', and that the government knew nothing. Witnesses interviewed by Bardakçı insist he was a patriot. They say Talat signed the execution order while Enver was away, and 'wept bitterly' as he did so.

## Interesting details

1. During the raid on the Porte he killed [[Nazım Paşa]], who shouted 'You deceived me!'
   > “Bu sırada, Nâzım Paşa küfrederek, “Siz beni aldattınız” diye çıkışırken Yakup Cemil tarafından öldürüldü. Söylentiye göre İT Nâzım’ı sadrazam yapma sözüyle onun desteğini elde etmişti.”
   > — [[Kısa Türkiye Tarihi (Sina Akşin)#loc. 55|Akşin, *Kısa Türkiye Tarihi*, loc. 55]] · *Turkish source*
2. Said Halim to the inquiry: 'Yakub Cemil was a madman; the government knew nothing of this.'
   > “Said Halim Paşa - Malum-ı aliniz Yakub Cemil, mecnunun biri idi ve bu meseleden hükumetin haberi yokdur. Şemseddin Bey (Ertuğrul) - Demek ki, bu iş, sırf askeri idi.”
   > — [[İttihad ve Terakki Yargılamaları I (Erol Şadi Erdinç)#p. 66|Erdinç, *İttihad ve Terakki Yargılamaları I*, p. 66]] · *Turkish source*
3. Talat 'wept bitterly' but signed his death sentence.
   > “Yakup Cemil esasen kendi şeyini almak istiyor, otoritesini. - Fakat Talât Paşa idam ettirtti deniyor Yakup Cemil'i. - Evet, işte, diyorum size, ağladı hüngür hüngür, imza etti idamını.”
   > — [[Talât Paşa'nın Evrak-ı Metrukesi (Murat Bardakçı)#p. 118|Bardakçı, *Talât Paşa'nın Evrak-ı Metrukesi*, p. 118]] · *Turkish source*
4. It is said Talat had him executed while Enver, who protected him, was away.
   > “Enver Paşa, Yakup Cemil'i tutuyormuş. Talât Paşa'nın Emniyet Müdürü'ne, îstanbul Emniyet Müdürü'ne bir rapor yaptırtıp, Enver Paşa yokken idam ettirdiği söyleniyor Yakup Cemil'i.”
   > — [[Talât Paşa'nın Evrak-ı Metrukesi (Murat Bardakçı)#p. 118|Bardakçı, *Talât Paşa'nın Evrak-ı Metrukesi*, p. 118]] · *Turkish source*
