## Summary

Samuel S. Cox was an American diplomat in [[İstanbul]] in 1885–1887 and author of *Bir Amerikan Diplomatının İstanbul Anıları* (1887), the American source cited in this vault as 'Cox'. His name appears in the vault only in the notes of his own book, so this note is mainly a pointer to that source (see [[Sources Ledger]]).
