## Summary

The Ottoman Navy (*Bahriye*) is covered in the vault by [[Hobart Paşa]]'s memoir (British view), Badem on the Crimean War, and [[Cemal Paşa]] as Navy Minister. Cemal describes the dreadnoughts *Sultan Osman* and *Reşadiye*, built in Britain and seized in 1914 (see [[Rauf Orbay]]). Apprentices were sent to German factories (Toprak). Its magazine *Risale-i Mevkute-i Bahriye* published war documents (Badem). Sultan Vahdettin was carried to [[Malta]] on a British warship at the Navy's expense (Bardakçı). See also [[Battle of Navarino (1827)]], [[Battle of Çeşme (1770)]] and [[Goeben and Breslau (1914)]].
