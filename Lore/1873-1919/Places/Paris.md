## Summary

Paris was a centre of Ottoman exile, diplomacy and learning. In 1867 Mustafa Fazıl Paşa sent his famous letter from Paris (Ortaylı). The 1856 Paris Congress followed the Crimean War, and the Islahat Fermanı was issued around it (Akşin). From early 1919 the Peace Conference met in Paris (Mantran; see [[Paris Peace Conference (1919)]]), and later Allied foreign ministers met there on the Near East (Goloğlu). Çelik compares [[İstanbul]]'s development with river cities like Paris.
