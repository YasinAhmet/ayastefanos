# İran

> **Place** · Period: [[_Ledger 1873-1919|1873-1919]] · Found in **52** sources on **628** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]


## Summary

İran (Persia) was the Ottomans' eastern neighbour and rival. Süleyman led his last campaign against Iran in 1553 (Mantran). Cox (American view) notes the Persian envoy Mohsin Han at an Ottoman ceremony. In 1920–21 the Bolsheviks considered driving British influence out of Iran (Bardakçı). Akşin quotes the view that 'Turkey can never be an Iran', and [[Kılıç Ali]] notes British propaganda there.
