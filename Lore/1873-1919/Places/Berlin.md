## Summary

Berlin was the German capital and, in 1878, host of the [[Congress of Berlin (1878)]], where Armenian claims were first discussed on 4 July 1878 (Gürün). Bardakçı says the CUP tied not only the army but the state's fate to Berlin. The Unionist leaders fled there after 1918, and [[Talat Paşa]] was killed there in 1921. The Sarre family villa was a meeting place for Berlin's elite (Bardakçı). Reisman (American view) describes refugees from Nazi Berlin, such as the conductor Ernst Praetorius, who came to Turkey.

## Interesting details

1. 4 July 1878: the Armenian request was first discussed at the Congress of Berlin.
   > “The request of the Armenians was examined at the Berlin Congress for the first time on 4 July 1878. (The Conference lasted for thirty-one days, and during this time twenty sessions were held.) However, the discussion did not start with the Armenian request, but with a proposal by the British delegate Lord Salisbury asking that the 16th Article of Ayastefanos be modified, and that the stipulation that the evacuation be dependent upon the reforms be annulled.”
   > — [[The Armenian File (Kâmuran Gürün)#p. 154|Gürün, *The Armenian File*, p. 154]] · *Turkish source*
2. The CUP had bound the state's destiny to Berlin.
   > “Dolaysıyla devletin sadece kurumlarını ve ordusunu değil, kaderini de Berlin'e başlamış olan İttihad ve Terakkinin, kendisinin tamamen karşisinda düşünenleri, şehzade bile olsalar hoş karşılamayacağ tabiidir ve öyle olur: Vahideddin, Sultan Reşad'ın veliahdı Yusuf İzeddin Efendi’nin 1916'nin 1 Şubatı’nda intiharndan sonra kendisinin veliahd ilân yaçar. Hareketleri takip edilmekte, hatta ziyaretçileri bile kontrol altında”
   > — [[Şahbaba (Murat Bardakçı)#p. 53|Bardakçı, *Şahbaba*, p. 53]] · *Turkish source*
3. The Sarre villa remained a meeting place of Berlin's notables.
   > “Sarre ailesinin villası, Birinci Dünya Savaşı'nın ardından da Berlin'in önde gelenlerinin buluşma mekâni olmaya devam etti. Almanya'nın önde gelen siyasetçileri ile Berlin'in yüksek sosyetesi, villanın eski senelerde olduğu gibi yine müdavimi idiler ve 1919'da kurulup 1933'te Adolf Hitler'in iktidara gelmesi ile son bulan Weimar Cumhuriyetinin önemli isimleri de hemen her akşam Villa Sarre'de biraraya geliyorlardı…”
   > — [[Enver (Murat Bardakçı)#p. 108|Bardakçı, *Enver*, p. 108]] · *Turkish source*
