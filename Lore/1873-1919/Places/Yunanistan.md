## Summary

Yunanistan (Greece) won independence after the [[Greek War of Independence (1821)]], and its Church separated from the Patriarchate (Ortaylı). Cox (American view) mocks its thirty-odd cabinet changes since 1833. Local notables threatened revolt if Preveza, Arta and Yanya were given to it (Ortaylı). In 1919–22 it occupied [[İzmir]] and fought the nationalists. Dimitrios Gounaris was prime minister twice, in 1915 and 1921–22 (Bardakçı). Later, Greece's 12-mile claim and Cyprus strained relations (Akşin).
