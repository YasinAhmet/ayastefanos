## Summary

Batum was the Black Sea port given to Russia in 1878 along with Artvin (Bardakçı). In November 1914 Hafız Hakkı persuaded Bronsart to support an attack on Batum (Hakkı Paşa). Batum's Muslims sent delegates to join the national movement, and Goloğlu says they never forgot their motherland. Enver wrote letters from Batum in 1921 (Bardakçı). On 1 May workers in Batum demonstrated against the British (Aralov, Russian view).
