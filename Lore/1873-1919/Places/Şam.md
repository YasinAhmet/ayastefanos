## Summary

Şam (Damascus) was the capital of Ottoman Syria. The Supreme Consultative Council of Damascus represented its notable families in the early years of centralisation (Emrence). Its strategic place made it a school centre for the region (Bouquet). Under [[Cemal Paşa]] it was the wartime command centre, and he quotes French consular documents seized there. In 1918 it fell and [[Faysal]]'s Arab government was set up (Allawi). Bardakçı quotes rumours that Cemal planned to make Damascus a capital and himself a sultan.
