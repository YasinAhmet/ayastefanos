## Summary

Üsküdar is [[İstanbul]]'s old Asian quarter. New ferry lines let its people commute to the city (Çelik). In 1854 a tekke sheikh's death there shows state control of dervish lodges (Ortaylı). Its gendarmerie commander Remzi was sentenced to death in 1920 along with [[Bursa]]'s governor (Akşin). A Hamidian harem agha was insulted there after 1908 (Akşin).
