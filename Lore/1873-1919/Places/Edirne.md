## Summary

Edirne (Adrianople) was the old capital in Thrace. Its fall and recapture in the [[Balkan Wars (1912-1913)]] were a turning point: Enver won the title 'conqueror of Edirne' (Bardakçı). [[Cemal Paşa]] argued that keeping Edirne mattered more than the islands. Trotsky (Russian view) reports talk comparing its defences with Kırkkilise. The 1829 Edirne treaty led many Armenians to move to Russia (Gürün). In October 1919 the acting governor reported national movements in Edirne and eastern Thrace (Goloğlu).
