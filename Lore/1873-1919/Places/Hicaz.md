## Summary

The Hicaz, with Mecca and Medina, came under the Ottomans with Selim I's conquest of Egypt in 1517; some argued the caliphate passed to the Ottomans from then (Ortaylı). The tribes between Ma'an and the Hicaz were so strong that railway passengers paid extra for protection (Emrence). The Ottoman garrison of about 2,000 men, including the governor, surrendered to Abdullah on 23 September 1916 (Allawi). An expeditionary force for the Hicaz was formed in the war ([[Kılıç Ali]]). After 1922 King Hüseyin ruled it; Vahdettin went there in exile but could not agree with him (Bardakçı).
