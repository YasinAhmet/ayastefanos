## Summary

Orientalism covers Western images of the East, studied in Onur Bilge Kula's *Batı Edebiyatında Oryantalizm*. The video transcripts quote Edward Said on the Ottoman threat to Christian civilisation until the 17th century. Ortaylı notes that Orientalism at least introduced these peoples, though with images of camels and harems. Bouquet describes Orientalist travellers' meetings with pashas. Akşin discusses [[Ahmed Rıza]] in a post-colonial frame. The Ottomans also used 'Orientalist' styles in their own buildings (Çelik).
