# St. Petersburg

> **Place** · Period: [[_Ledger 18th Century|18th Century]] · Found in **41** sources on **615** pages · [[Home]] · [[Timeline]]

## Summary

St. Petersburg was the capital of the [[Russian Empire]]. It was a centre of Russian Orientalist scholarship on Islam and the Caucasus (Ortaylı). Pan-Slavist meetings there and at Prague in 1908 launched a new movement (Trotsky, Russian view). [[Mizancı Murad]] was educated there (Akşin). In March 1917 the war's hardships led to street riots in Petrograd, starting the revolution (Akşin; see [[Russian Revolution (1917)]]).
